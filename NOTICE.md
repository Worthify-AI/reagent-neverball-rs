Worthify Rolling Lab is an independent, bounded reimplementation of one Neverball training course. Our code and converted numeric course fixture are distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later.

Neverball is copyright Robert Kooima and contributors and is GPL-2.0-or-later. The Easy 01 course is by Robert Kooima. The fixture in `fixtures/course.json` was converted from the licensed distribution's compiled SOL data, with original materials and UV coordinates restored for presentation. Original game implementation source and existing reimplementation source were withheld during construction. Public help, license information, the executable, its decompiler output, compiled level data, runtime observations and recorded inputs were used. Familiar mechanics and possible model prior knowledge were not eliminated.

We redistribute the licensed Easy 01 visual assets, basic ball, coin, cloud/hill backdrop and unmodified DejaVu Sans Bold font in assets/neverball. Original JPEGs and compiled SOL data are retained as the sources for PNG conversion and numeric visual fixtures. Corresponding preferred-form .map, .obj, .wings and material files are retained under assets/neverball/source, with versioned source URLs in SOURCE-ASSETS.json. Asset-specific license terms remain in force; the general Neverball assets are GPL-2.0-or-later. No executable, audio or Octocat resources are included. The reference recording in the companion article shows the unmodified licensed game and credits Neverball; it is not a proprietary Windows XP game.

Upstream: https://neverball.org/ and https://github.com/Neverball/neverball/blob/master/LICENSE.md

The presentation uses licensed reference art with independently authored rendering code. Lighting, transparency, shadows, camera/background alignment, HUD gradients, effects and ball rolling orientation are approximations. This is a bounded course reconstruction, not an exact visual or complete engine reproduction. No recorded reference position is injected into the simulation.
