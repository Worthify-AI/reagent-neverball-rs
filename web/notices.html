Runtime-captured checker ball

A later experiment recovered the default checker-ball texture from the running reference through OpenGL. The ordinary Rust material loader uses this captured derivative, including its alpha of 127. Other artwork remains the separately disclosed licensed asset reuse. An original, independently authored helper was compiled and launched by ReAgent chat; after capture ended, comparison with licensed assets identified the checker ball and its vertical orientation.
